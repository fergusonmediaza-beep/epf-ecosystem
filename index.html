Why 2026?

2026 is not just a year on the EPF roadmap — it is Africa's economic inflection point. For the first time in history, Africa's working-age population will overtake the rest of the world combined. This is the window. Miss it, and the continent loses a generation. Seize it, and the return — financial and social — is unprecedented.
