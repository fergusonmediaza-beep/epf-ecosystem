Privacy Policy

How EPF Ecosystem collects, uses, and protects the information you provide through our website and contact form.

Contents
Information We Collect How We Use It EmailJS & Transmission Data Sharing Data Retention Your Rights Security Cookies Contact Us

Last updated: 30 April 2026  •  Empire Partner Foundation NPC — 35 Ferguson Road, Illovo, Sandton, Johannesburg, 2196, South Africa

1 Information We Collect

When you submit a message through our contact form, we collect the following information you voluntarily provide:

  • First name and last name
  • Business email address
  • Phone number (optional)
  • Organisation or company name (optional)
  • Your role or title (optional)
  • Your message and the nature of your enquiry (partnership, investment, solutions, government procurement, or general)

We do not collect identity documents, banking details, passwords, or any sensitive personal information through our website contact form.

2 How We Use Your Information

The information you submit is used exclusively to:

  • Route your enquiry to the correct team within the EPF Ecosystem
  • Respond to your message and follow up on partnership, investment, or solution requests
  • Maintain an internal record of communications for continuity of service

Your information is never used for unsolicited marketing, sold to third parties, or shared outside of the EPF Ecosystem team without your explicit consent.

3 EmailJS & Data Transmission

Our contact form uses EmailJS — a third-party email delivery service — to transmit your submitted information securely to our internal team at info@epfecosystem.co.za.

When you submit the form, your data is sent via EmailJS's servers and delivered directly to our inbox. EmailJS processes data in accordance with their own privacy policy, which can be reviewed at emailjs.com/legal/privacy-policy.

We do not store your contact data on our own servers. The information resides in our email account and any internal records maintained by our team.

4 Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. Your data is shared only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Within EPF Ecosystem: Members of our team who handle your specific enquiry on a need-to-know basis
  • EmailJS: As the technical service used to deliver your message to us (see Section 3)
  • Legal obligation: If required by applicable South African law or a valid court order

If your enquiry progresses to a formal partnership or investment process, any further information shared at that stage will be governed by a Non-Disclosure Agreement.

5 Data Retention

Contact data is retained for a period of 12 months from the date of submission. After this period, data is securely deleted unless an active partnership, investment, or business relationship has been established — in which case data is retained for the duration of that relationship and for a further period as required by applicable law.

You may request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us using the details in Section 9.

6 Your Rights

In accordance with the Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) of South Africa, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Object to the processing of your information
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details in Section 9.

7 Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse. Our website is served over HTTPS and all form data is transmitted via encrypted channels through EmailJS.

However, no transmission over the internet is completely secure. While we strive to protect your information, we cannot guarantee absolute security of data transmitted through our website.

8 Cookies

Our website uses minimal cookies necessary for basic site functionality. We do not use tracking cookies, advertising cookies, or third-party analytics that collect personal information without consent.

Google Fonts are loaded from Google's CDN, which may set a cookie in accordance with Google's privacy policy. Font Awesome icons are loaded from Cloudflare's CDN. No other third-party cookies are in use on this site.
